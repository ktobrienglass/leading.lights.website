Miscellaneous

✦

A curious category but we need to place another dimension on this craft, including people who work in it. We involve craftsmen and women we have worked with in the past and present, who Leading Lights of London Ltd would like to mention for sharing their special knowledge and experience. We placed the Rules restaurant project in this category as it has such a unique character and they serve some of the best cocktails in London. We also recognise the special contribution made by John Wilson to the design of our website.
