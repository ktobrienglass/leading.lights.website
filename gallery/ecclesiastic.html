Ecclesiastic

✦

Church Stained Glass. The beauty of stained glass is instantly recognisable in this form. Church stained glass windows depict celestial bodies, saints, church patrons, biblical characters and animals. It comes into its own in our churches and chapels around the capital. Often involving the church architect, church warden or the vicar, we consult and ensure that all church stained glass windows, repairs and refurbishment are carried out to the highest standards. We are able to repair damaged stained glass and clear leaded lights, often carrying out 'drop-ins' that involves taking out individual cracked glass and replacing them on site. Stained glass cleaning is also undertaken.
